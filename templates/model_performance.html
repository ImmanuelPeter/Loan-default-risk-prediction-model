What Each Evaluation Metric Measures

  • Accuracy ({{ (evaluations[0].accuracy*100)|round(1) if evaluations else '88' }}%):
    The overall proportion of correct predictions across all non-default and default loan cases.
  • Precision:
    Among applicants predicted to default, the proportion who actually defaulted (minimizes false alarms).
  • Recall (Sensitivity):
    Among applicants who actually defaulted, the proportion correctly identified (guards bank capital).
  • F1-Score:
    The harmonic mean of precision and recall, balancing false approvals and false denials.
  • ROC-AUC:
    Measures ranking ability to distinguish between default and solvent borrowers across all cutoff thresholds.
