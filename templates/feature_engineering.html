Debt-to-Income (DTI)

Proportion of gross monthly earnings committed to existing obligations plus the new installment.

DTI = (Existing Debt + Installment) / Income × 100%
Underwriting Limit: ≤ 40% (Acceptable) | > 50% (High Delinquency)
LEVERAGE MULTIPLIER Importance: 16.5%

Loan-to-Income (LTI)

Facility principal relative to monthly earnings, indicating total borrower burden.

LTI = Loan Principal / Monthly Income
Underwriting Limit: ≤ 4.0× (Healthy) | > 6.0× (Overextended)
CASHFLOW LIQUIDITY Importance: 13.0%

Installment-to-Income (ITI)

Monthly amortization obligation of the proposed loan taken alone against monthly earnings.

ITI = Proposed Monthly Installment / Monthly Income × 100%
Underwriting Limit: ≤ 30% (Safe) | > 40% (Restricted)
SHOCK ABSORBER Importance: 9.5%

Disposable Surplus

Residual cash left for living expenses and emergencies after all monthly debt service.

Surplus = Monthly Income - (Existing Debt + Installment)
Minimum Standard: ≥ MWK 150,000 residual buffer
