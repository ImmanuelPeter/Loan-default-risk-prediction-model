About us

Better credit decisions begin with useful data

Manual loan assessment can be slow, subjective, and inconsistent. The Loan Default Risk Model transforms approved borrower, application, and financial information into a clear default-risk estimate.

The platform helps financial institutions identify potential risk earlier, compare applications consistently, and keep trained people responsible for the final lending decision.

Built around responsible risk assessment

  • Uses a trained machine-learning model with visible probability and risk class.
  • Supports data-informed review without replacing credit officers.
  • Tracks prediction history, lending decisions, and actual loan outcomes.
  • Separates company workspaces and restricts access to authorized users.

How it works

From registration to prediction

Follow one structured process while preserving human oversight at every lending decision.

1 Register the institution

Submit company information and administrator details for platform approval.

2 Set up borrowers

Add or select borrowers and record approved financial and application information.

3 Generate a prediction

The trained model returns default probability, risk category, and prediction details.

4 Review and report

Make the lending decision, record outcomes, and compare performance through reports.

Security and data protection

Protect information across every workspace

Data protection is built into how the platform separates institutions, authorizes users, and records important actions.

OI
Organization isolation

Company records are scoped to their institution so unauthorized workspaces cannot access them.

RA
Role-based access

Company and employee functions are limited through assigned roles and authorized account access.

AU
Important activity records

Operational events help administrators review significant changes, decisions, and platform activity.

HR
Human review

Predictions remain decision-support information and do not automatically approve or reject a loan.
