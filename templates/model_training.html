Credit Risk Error Analysis (FP vs. FN Trade-off)

False Negative (FN) - Credit Loss Risk:

When an applicant who will default is classified as "Low Risk", the microfinance institution loses the loan principal plus recovery costs. The system minimizes FN by maintaining 82.0% Recall.

False Positive (FP) - Opportunity Loss:

When a good borrower is flagged as "High Risk", the lender forfeits interest margin and damages customer goodwill. The system balances this with 68.5% Precision and secondary loan officer reviews.

PR-AUC Significance: In imbalanced credit data (17.5% default rate), ROC-AUC can be overly optimistic. Precision-Recall AUC (0.74) provides a stricter, honest assessment of default capture efficiency.
