Module 2 Ingestion Specifications

Your uploaded records are stored in your personal workspace and checked before they are added to your loan data:

  • Duplicate Prevention: Applicant information is checked to reduce accidental duplicate records.
  • Personal data: Applicant records and loans are kept separate from other user accounts.
  • Automatic ID Generation: Generates unique applicant identifiers and loan codes for your records.
  • Missing Value Safeguards: Any blank debt or employment values are routed through the Module 3 Preprocessing engine.
